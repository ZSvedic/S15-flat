# Hrnetička: interior spec

Version 1, written from Zel's voice notes on 2026-10-19.
The source of truth is `S15-Hrneticka.sh3d`, a Sweet Home 3D model with walls traced from the developer's plan to about ±5 cm.

## Goal

- Furnish S15 in the Sweet Home 3D model to see how it will look and to check that everything has enough space.
- Most of the current furniture stays in the current flat (B-4-3). S15 gets **new furniture**: real European products or made-to-measure pieces, at their real sizes.
- Zel's last change to the model: he flipped the living-room sofa so the long side is against the east wall. That model only checked room sizes; the new design replaces it.

Room numbers are from the developer's plan.

## 1. Living room + kitchen (room 8, 39,8 m²)

**TV**
- Keep the TV in the same place on the north wall.
- Get a bigger TV.

**Sofa**
- Get a bigger sofa with backrests on both the east side and the south side, so a full corner sofa rather than one with a chaise.

**Coffee table**
- Get a slightly bigger one.

**Work desk**
- Zel and Inge both work from home, so the living room also needs a work desk.

**Kitchen**
- Design it properly, using the same colours as the current kitchen: grey, wood and cream.

**Dining: two options, model both**
- A. A smaller dining table plus a kitchen island. The floor already has installations for an island.
- B. A larger dining table and no island.

## 2. Master bedroom (room 3, 12,7 m²)

- The wardrobe on the west wall is in the wrong place: it blocks the door to the ensuite bathroom. Move it further south.
- Remove the small cabinet (BESTA) on the south side. There isn't enough space for it.
- Use standard-size nightstands.

## 3. Hallway (room 1)

- Add a made-to-measure built-in wardrobe for storage.
- Remove the IKEA NORDLI chests.
- Replace them with a simple bench that has drawers or shoe shelves underneath.

## 4. Laundry / technical room (room 7)

- Move the washer and the dryer further east, because the heat pump unit is on the west side of the room.

## 5. Sara's room (room 6, 10,6 m², has the terrace door)

- This is mainly Sara's room; she is 17.
- The room is too small for both a bed and a sofa. Options:
  - A. A multifunction bed that converts into a couch with backrests (daybed or sofa bed).
  - B. A normal bed plus a small reading chair or loveseat by the south window.
- She still needs a desk and a wardrobe.

## 6. Multifunction room (room 5, 9,9 m²)

- On most days it's a workroom.
- Sometimes it's a guest room: for guests, or for overnight stays by Inge's kids (a girl and a boy, 9 and 12).
- So it needs both a work setup and a way to sleep guests that takes up little space when not in use.

## 7. North balcony (terrace 10, 18,7 m²)

- The parapet wall is about 1,2 m high.
- It's mainly for summer: coffee and the view north to Sljeme.

## 8. East–south terrace (terrace 9, 46,2 m²)

The terrace is used all year and should be designed properly.

**Furniture**
- Large outdoor furniture.
- Maybe an outdoor barbecue.
- Maybe an outdoor sink.

**Greenery**
- Small evergreen trees in well-designed planters, for example a silver pine, Pinus nigra, or small olive trees.

**Storage**
- A box for cushions and anything else that shouldn't get wet straight away.

**Shade: two options**
- A. A fixed pergola with open vertical slats that shade the terrace for most of the day, especially in summer, but let air and rain through.
- B. One or two retractable fabric sails or awnings covering half or all of the terrace:
  - with a rain sensor, so they extend when it starts to rain;
  - with a wind sensor, so they retract above a set wind speed to protect the construction.

## 9. Flooring for the terrace and balcony

Two options:
- A. Wooden decking boards in a "navy" style, like on a sailboat.
- B. High-quality artificial grass that looks like grass from a distance, is low-maintenance, and can be cleaned by an outdoor robot vacuum.

## Open questions

1. **TV:** what screen size (e.g. 75" or 85")?
2. **Sofa:** roughly how big, and should it be a modular brand? Its south backrest sits next to the terrace sliding door, so how much walking space is needed?
3. **Living-room desk:** one shared desk or two? What size?
4. **Kitchen:** keep the current layout (a column of units against the bedroom wall plus a short run along the north wall), or redesign it? Which appliances should it have?
5. **Master bed:** a new bed? 160 or 180 cm wide?
6. **Hallway wardrobe:** where exactly — the niche by the front door, or along the corridor?
7. **Heat pump unit:** what size is it, and exactly where does it go?
8. **Multifunction room:** how should guests sleep — a wall bed with a desk built in, a sofa bed, or bunks? How many desks?
9. **Terrace water:** is there a water tap on the terrace, or only a drain? A sink needs a water supply.
10. **Pergola or sails:** does the building or the co-owners need to approve it? Who decides?

## Notes from the model

These are measurements and observations, not decisions.

- **Master bedroom:** the ensuite door takes up about 380–460 cm along the west wall, and the room ends at 650 cm. That leaves 190 cm south of the door, so a 200 cm wardrobe won't fit there. It would need to be 190 cm or narrower.
- **Living room:** with the current sofa, the gap to the terrace-door wall is 60 cm. A deeper south backrest makes that gap smaller.
- **Sails:** rain often comes with wind. A sail that extends when it rains but retracts above a wind threshold won't protect the terrace in stormy rain.
