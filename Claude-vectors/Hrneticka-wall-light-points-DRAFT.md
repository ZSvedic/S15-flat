## Switching and dimming (Zel, 2026-11-26: three groups; 2026-11-28: four ambient dimmers)
1. **Ambient**: all wall lights, picture lights, TV pucks and shelf up-light, wardrobe-top up-lights, kitchen bar, dining pendant, low-lamp sockets, hall seat bar, niche. **Four dimmers** (Zel, 2026-11-28):
   - A: living room + kitchen + hall + corridor (5–9, 11, 18, 19, K, D, L1–L3);
   - B: master bedroom (1–3, 20, L4);
   - C: Sara (15, 16, 21);
   - D: guest room / room 5 (12, 13).
   In the renders they are still one group (one slider).
2. **Ceiling**: all ceiling lamps and spots, desk work light, mirror border lights (M1, M2). On/off; scene 2 = ambient + ceiling. Laundry wall light 10 and the terrace lamps have their own switches; the renders put them in this group.
3. **Bed switches** (Zel, 2026-11-28): a wired push button in a box at each bed side, for local off of 2, 3 (bedroom), 12 (guest room) and 15 (Sara).
3. **Day**: no lamps.

Dimming is by smart dimmers in the wall boxes (decided 2026-11-28, row 12 below). Ask for a neutral wire and a deep box at each dimmer position. Test the dimmer with the real LEDARE lamps first: small LED loads can flicker. The PILSKOTT lamps are dimmed by their own IKEA remote, not by the wall dimmer.
